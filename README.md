# 🌱 Carbon-Xray

### Digital Carbon Intelligence for Individuals & Enterprises

Carbon-Xray is a sustainability-focused platform exploring how AI can make carbon footprint analysis, carbon accounting, and compliance workflows more accessible.

The platform provides two experiences: a Digital Shadow Scanner for individuals and an Invoice-to-Audit System for enterprises.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Carbon data is often scattered across digital activity, invoices, purchase orders, and operational records.

Carbon-Xray explores how AI can transform these raw data sources into structured carbon intelligence through automated analysis and document processing.

Digital Activity / Business Documents
              ↓
       AI Data Extraction
              ↓
       Carbon Intelligence
              ↓
     Compliance & Insights

---

## 👤 Digital Shadow Scanner

The Digital Shadow Scanner focuses on the hidden environmental impact of everyday digital activity.

Users can upload screenshots from their device's digital wellbeing or screen-time statistics and explore the estimated carbon impact of their digital habits.

### Supported Inputs

- iOS Screen Time screenshots
- Android Digital Wellbeing screenshots
- Demo data for quick exploration

### Workflow

Screen-Time Screenshot
        ↓
   Data Extraction
        ↓
 Digital Activity Analysis
        ↓
 Estimated Carbon Impact

---

## 🏢 Invoice-to-Audit System

The enterprise experience focuses on turning procurement documents into structured carbon and compliance data.

Users can upload business documents such as GST invoices and purchase orders for AI-assisted extraction and analysis.

### Supported Documents

- PDF
- JPG
- PNG
- GST Invoices
- Purchase Orders

The system is designed around carbon accounting and compliance workflows, including use cases related to:

- EU CBAM
- India CCTS
- Procurement carbon analysis
- Carbon data extraction
- Audit preparation

### Workflow

GST Invoice / Purchase Order
             ↓
        Document Upload
             ↓
      AI-Assisted Extraction
             ↓
       Material / Data Analysis
             ↓
      Carbon & Compliance Data

## 🎯 Product Vision

Carbon-Xray explores a future where carbon accounting doesn't have to depend entirely on manual spreadsheets and fragmented data.

The goal is to create intelligent systems that can understand information from different sources and turn it into structured, actionable carbon intelligence.

> From raw business data to carbon intelligence.
